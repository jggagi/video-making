# Scientific grounding and metaphor boundaries

Checked against primary sources on 2026-10-19.

- Lean's trusted kernel checks formal proof terms relative to the selected type theory and axioms. It does not establish that the external-world specification is correct; compilation alone is a weaker and different guarantee. Source: [Theorem Proving in Lean, Introduction](https://lean-lang.org/theorem_proving_in_lean4/Introduction/).
- A learned reward is an imperfect proxy; strong optimization can raise proxy scores while harming the gold objective in the studied synthetic RLHF setup. This supports the closed-loop risk metaphor, not a claim all self-play fails. Source: [Gao, Schulman & Hilton, 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.10760).
- Indiscriminate recursive use of model-generated training data can lose distribution tails. This is a training-data feedback phenomenon, distinct from reward hacking. Source: [Shumailov et al., Nature 2024, corrected article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07566-y).
- The 10-year experiment, SI subjective time, generation numbers, score, parameter counts, energy and broken cell are hypothetical cinematic telemetry. No claim of a real clinical result or measured SI state is made. One million times faster would turn ten years into ten million subjective years. The requested hundred-million-year HUD corresponds to ten million times, while “一秒万年” is poetic exaggeration and arithmetically incompatible with either literal multiplier. All requested expressions are preserved: the Act 2 live elapsed counter shows 10,000,000 years while the HUD/narration describe a 100,000,000-year full wait; the counter is an illustrative intermediate state, not a second conversion rule. A visible hypothetical multiplier qualifier applies to the full-wait HUD.
- “一万亿倍” and the cathedral's 10^14 parameters versus the brief's “10-trillion-parameter” collapse are not empirical counts. They stay in the original text and are documented as fictional scale language.
- The final line uses Landauer's lower bound for logically irreversible erasure, ΔQ ≥ k_B T ln2; it is not a universal heat cost per arbitrary computation.
