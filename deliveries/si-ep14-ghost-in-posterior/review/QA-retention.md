# QA retention

Git retains scan reports and per-frame metrics, compact encoded mobile contacts, native field/spore crops, frame405 redraw and encoded-neighbor evidence, measurements and review judgments. Bulk native-frame extractions and source stills remain local and are ignored here; the native final MP4 and rejected MP4 are preserved in Releases and the extraction indices/tool are versioned. They can be reconstructed by decoding those specific movie hashes. Rejected source-code/texture candidates remain preserved. No failed report or candidate movie was removed or relabeled as passed.
