# Scientific basis and narrative limits

The episode is a science-fiction thought experiment. Its dramatic compression, invented telemetry, final equation, and “unknown remaining: 0” are not scientific results. The original narration and promotional copy remain intact per the production decision; these notes qualify the claims for production and review.

## Action and the displayed identity

The stationarity condition `δS = 0` is a real variational principle used to derive equations of motion from an action. The displayed expression is not evidence that one final law has unified all known physics, and “stationary” does not always mean a global minimum. Richard Feynman's Caltech lecture describes the action principle and its relation to Newtonian mechanics: [The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Vol. II, Chapter 19](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/II_19.html).

## Kolmogorov complexity and the HUD counters

Kolmogorov's algorithmic description-length framework is real ([Kolmogorov, 1965](https://www.mathnet.ru/php/archive.phtml?jrnid=ppi&option_lang=eng&paperid=68&wshow=paper)). Program-size complexity is not computable in general; see Chaitin's foundational paper, [“On the Length of Programs for Computing Finite Binary Sequences” (1966)](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/321356.321363). Exact bit counts also depend on the chosen universal description language up to an additive machine-dependent constant. Therefore `10^18 BITS -> 128 BITS`, `14,820`, `99.94%`, and the smooth countdown are fictional interface values, not measurements of nature or an actual compression run.

## Newton, Ptolemy, and epicycles

The original narration's image of Newton erasing “上百个繁琐的圆圈” is retained as a dramatic historical simplification, not a literal account. Kepler established the elliptical planetary orbits that departed from circular constructions; Newton later used Kepler's laws in developing universal gravitation. NASA's history recounts that sequence ([Planetary Motion: The History of an Idea That Launched the Scientific Revolution](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/planetary-motion/)). A Smithsonian historical study notes the substantial role of Copernicus and Kepler in simplifying epicycle models ([Early History, Smithsonian Annals of Flight](https://repository.si.edu/bitstream/handle/10088/18680/SAoF-0010-Lo_res.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=2)). The narration and caption must not be silently rewritten; the visual may present the comparison as an explicitly fictional compression metaphor.

## Unification, quantum gravity, and life

There is no established result in this production that unifies general relativity, quantum physics, thermodynamics, biology, and mind into one equation. The four panels aligning is an artistic event, not evidence that these fields have been reconciled. NASA describes gravity as outside the Standard Model's account of the other fundamental interactions and frames quantum-gravity work as an active research problem ([NASA SUPREME-QG](https://www.nasa.gov/directorates/stmd/niac/niac-studies/supreme-qg-space-borne-ultra-precise-measurement-of-the-equivalence-principle-signature-of-quantum-gravity/)).

The source's `Q.E.D.`, “宇宙全部定律已证明完毕,” `RESIDUAL MYSTERY: 0.000%`, and “未知剩余: 0” are fictional end-state telemetry. They must not be described in the production notes or publication package as observed conclusions. Keep the approved final-frame disclaimer exactly as timed in `timeline.json`.

## What the four graphic panels actually compute

The gravity panel uses Marching Squares on a softened planar scalar potential from three point sources. The quantum panel is an artistic radial/azimuthal nodal rose with deterministic density stippling; it is not a normalized numerical hydrogen eigenstate or a probability simulation. The flow panel integrates a chosen 2D vector field and constructs tangent arrowheads; it does not solve the general Navier–Stokes equations. The life panel is a binary branching geometry, not a model of biological metabolism or consciousness. Their conformal translations/scales and visual overlap are authored design, not a symbolic-regression result. The interface counters are not calculated compression performance.
