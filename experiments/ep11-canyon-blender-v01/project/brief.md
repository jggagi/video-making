## Image and action

A macro camera travels through a fissure in thick, broken ivory porcelain. The fracture has a physical bisque interior and rounded glaze edges, rather than a generic rocky height field. A substantial small shard falls, holds for a beat, and rises while a narrow pool of gold runs into the fissure. The memorable event must remain understandable without narration.

Palette: luminous ivory glaze and warm unglazed fracture, deep desaturated blue/teal recesses, metallic amber gold. Lighting: large soft side key, warm grazing back light, negative fill; actual Cycles contact and cast shadows, reflections and restrained depth of field. Avoid muddy plastic, rectangular paper particles, floating painted lines, giant emissive ribbons, and a uniformly dark frame.
