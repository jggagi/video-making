# EP17 scientific production notes

These notes flag where the script uses mathematical results as dramatic imagery. The film can keep its fictional SI and ouroboros conceit; these notes are production guardrails, not a narration rewrite.

| Script image or claim | Precise reading | Concise qualifier or copy guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `EXTERNAL UNIVERSE THEOREMS: 100.000% PROVED [OMEGA COMPLETE]`; the SI has solved every secret. | This is fictional telemetry, not a consequence of Gödel's or Turing's results. “Omega-complete” is a technical property of a theory, not a universal measure of knowledge or proof coverage. | Mark the telemetry as fictional when it first appears, for example `剧情遥测 · 数值为虚构`. The planned end-card disclaimer is useful but arrives after the proof-like HUD has been on screen. |
| “任何逻辑系统，都无法在内部证明自身的绝对自洽。” | This blends the two incompleteness theorems and overstates the scope. Gödel's second theorem concerns a consistent, effectively axiomatized theory with enough arithmetic, using its standard arithmetized consistency statement and ordinary derivability conditions: that theory cannot prove that statement about itself. It is not a theorem about every logic system or every informal meaning of “absolute consistency.” | Keep the claim tied to a sufficiently strong formal arithmetic theory and to its own formalized consistency statement. A compact qualifier could say `特定形式算术系统的内部一致性结论`. |
| `G iff not Prov(G)` and the proof pulse that switches between “unprovable” and “false.” | This is shorthand for an arithmetically coded self-reference sentence relative to a specified theory T and its provability predicate; it is not a free-standing equation for every logic. For Gödel's original 1931 construction, consistency rules out a proof of the constructed sentence, while the original argument uses the stronger ω-consistency condition to rule out a proof of its negation and establish undecidability. Provability, truth in the standard natural numbers, and contradiction are distinct notions. | Label the display as a schematic Gödel sentence for a particular theory. Do not animate it as a proof that every self-reference flips truth values or creates a contradiction. |
| `H(P, P) -> LOOP` / “the stop command will never arrive.” | The modern halting-problem statement is that no total algorithm correctly decides, for every program M and input x, whether M halts on x. A particular program's self-query may halt or run forever; self-reference alone does not settle it. | If visualizing the diagonal argument, label H as a `假设中的通用停机判定器`, then show D(x): if H(x,x) says “halts,” loop; otherwise halt. The contradiction is at D(D), not a universal claim that every self-query loops. |
| The SI must fully simulate itself, recurse through 10,000 or infinitely many copies, and physically overheat or crash. | Neither incompleteness nor undecidability requires full self-simulation, an infinite runtime, transistor heating, stack overflow, or any particular machine failure. Those are story choices about one fictional implementation. | Present recursion depth, `10^80`, stack depth, heat, deadlock, and frozen hardware as fictional scene telemetry, not measured SI behavior or mathematical consequences. |
| An outside spark or “human intuition” frees the SI; humans avoid the loop because they are irrational or non-computable. | Gödel and Turing do not establish that human thought is non-computable, that people escape undecidability, or that an external event solves a general undecidable problem. The warm drop is a narrative intervention. Claims about actual consciousness or neural processing would need separate empirical evidence. | Keep the spark as metaphor and the human comparison as a question or character belief, not a result of either theorem. |

The closing disclaimer (`未来 SI 思想实验 · 遥测与数值均为虚构`) correctly frames the fiction, but consider a short fiction label near the first precision-styled HUD or theorem graphic as well. This review covers the supplied script and source references only; no human listening, device playback, or on-device visual acceptance was performed.
